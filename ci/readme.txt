├── Makefile                 your build entry (make clean / make all)

├── ci\                      all CI/build scripts (version-controlled)

│   ├── fetch_deps.bat       svn export deps -> X:\deps           (X: guard)
│   ├── build.bat            make + deterministic log             (X: guard)
│   ├── ci.bat               fetch_deps + build, single entry     (X: guard)
│   ├── make_docs.bat        documentation step (placeholder)
│   └── package.bat          ZIP+PDF packaging (placeholder)
│
├── src\ ...                 your sources (whatever the Makefile builds)
